I’m a Product Designer who’s curious about people, problems, and the little things we often overlook. I tend to notice the small difficulties I face while doing something and immediately start wondering, “Why does it have to be this way?” I ask a lot of questions, probably more than necessary, but it helps me look at problems from different perspectives. Being an empath also helps me understand how the same experience might feel completely different to someone else.

When I’m not designing, I’m probably dancing, reading a good book, or getting distracted by a cat. I like noticing things, questioning them, and turning those little observations into ideas.
